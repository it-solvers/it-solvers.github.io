Who runs this site

IT Solvers is an IT service business run by its CEO, Hyeok Lee (Business Registration No. 364-11-03265). We take on software, web, and mobile app development, system maintenance, and IT asset and PC supply and installation.

Today we build web and mobile apps together with AI, and we write up the process on this site one piece at a time. Before that, the CEO spent many years building finance and lease business systems, and the lease content here draws on what we learned then.

This site

What you will find here

Technical insights

The English home page lists everything we publish for free:

  • Guides and terminology – a lease overview, lease terminology, and a guide to revenue recognition for finance and operating leases, explained in plain English with examples.
  • Calculators – a lease payment calculator, a cash-flow IRR calculator, and a calculator that works backward from a target IRR. The calculation methods are published with the code.
  • MVP (Minimum Viable Product) – services we are building ourselves, such as an e-contract tool you install on your own server with no subscription.

How we write

  • Accounting and lease content is explained against standards such as K-IFRS 1116, and we try to separate what the standard says from common industry practice.
  • Example figures are made up to aid understanding. The calculators are for learning only and are not an estimate, accounting opinion, or tax advice.
  • When we correct an error, we fix the page and show the publication date at the top.
  • If you find a mistake, please tell us through the contact page. We will review it and update the page.

Process

From Request to Delivery

We work in this order: estimate, contract, tax documents, delivery and inspection, and payment confirmation.

01

Estimate

We confirm your request, then send you an estimate.

02

Contract (e-contract or paper)

Once you confirm the estimate, we move on to the contract.

03

Tax documents

We issue an electronic tax invoice or a cash receipt.

04

Installation, delivery, and inspection

We deliver the products, then handle any needed installation, initial setup, and final inspection.

05

Payment

Once delivery and inspection are done, we confirm your payment.

06

Wrap-up

We review the delivery once more and close out the deal.
