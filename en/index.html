The lease business, explained clearly

Clear guides to the lease business and its terminology, plus calculators to help you work through lease payments and cash flows. Calculator amounts are shown in U.S. dollars ($); the guides describe K-IFRS and are not U.S. GAAP guidance.

Guides and terminology

Lease terminology

Plain-English explanations of residual value, lease payments, the implicit rate, initial direct costs, and related terms.

Lease business overview

A concise introduction to lease identification, lessee accounting, lessor classification, and common lease structures under K-IFRS 1116.

Supporting calculators

Lease cash-flow IRR calculator

Model monthly receipts and the timing of insurance and vehicle-tax payments to estimate an annualized cash-flow IRR.

Back-solve a lease payment from a target IRR

Use a bisection search to estimate the base monthly lease payment that matches a target cash-flow IRR.

Lease payment calculator

Estimate a monthly payment from the asset price, term, annual rate, and end-of-term residual value.

Important: These tools are for illustration and learning, not a quote, accounting opinion, tax advice, or a substitute for reviewing the applicable contract and standards. The lease guides are based on K-IFRS 1116 and may differ from U.S. GAAP and U.S. tax rules.
