Calculators

Lease cash-flow IRR calculator ↗

Enter the monthly receipts and when insurance and vehicle tax are paid to get an annualized cash-flow IRR.

Find a lease payment from a target IRR ↗

Start from a target cash-flow IRR and work backward to the base monthly lease payment with a bisection search.

Lease payment calculator ↗

Estimate a monthly payment from the vehicle price, term, annual rate, and residual value at the end of the term.

MVP (Minimum Viable Product)

E-Contract ↗

E-signatures without a subscription, for people who'd rather not leave their contracts on someone else's platform. Install it on your own server to keep your contracts for good. The other party just signs on their phone from a single link.

Get a Lease Estimate (in progress) ↗

Build a lease estimate from the vehicle, term, and interest rate. It's still in development and only available in Korean.

Accounting for Developers ↗

A basic accounting tool that helps you get comfortable with account titles through quizzes. The tool itself is in Korean only.

Important: These tools are for illustration and learning only. They aren't an estimate, accounting opinion, or tax advice, and they don't replace the actual contract or the applicable standards. The lease guides are based on K-IFRS 1116 and may differ from U.S. GAAP and U.S. tax rules.
