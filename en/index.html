Guides and terminology

Lease revenue recognition guide ↗

Compare how a lessor recognizes revenue and records journal entries for finance and operating leases, with a 36-month worked example and a vehicle-sale entry. Amounts are in U.S. dollars.

Lease terminology ↗

Plain-English explanations of residual value, lease payments, the implicit rate, initial direct costs, and more.

Lease business overview ↗

A short introduction to identifying a lease, lessee accounting, lessor classification, and common lease structures under K-IFRS 1116.

Calculators

Lease cash-flow IRR calculator ↗

Model monthly receipts and the timing of insurance and vehicle tax payments to estimate an annualized cash-flow IRR.

Find a lease payment from a target IRR ↗

Work backward from a target cash-flow IRR to the base monthly lease payment, using a bisection search.

Lease payment calculator ↗

Estimate a monthly payment from the vehicle price, term, annual interest rate, and residual value at the end of the term.

MVP (Minimum Viable Product)

E-Contract ↗

E-signatures without a subscription, for people who would rather not leave their contracts on someone else's platform. Install it on your own server to keep your contracts for good, and the other party signs on their phone from a single link.

Get a Lease Estimate (in progress) ↗

Build a lease estimate from the vehicle, term, and interest rate. Currently in development, and available in Korean only.

Important: These tools are for illustration and learning only. They are not an estimate, accounting opinion, or tax advice, and they don't replace reading the actual contract and the applicable standards. The lease guides are based on K-IFRS 1116 and may differ from U.S. GAAP and U.S. tax rules.
