What is this tool?

It is a basic accounting learning tool that helps you get used to account titles through quizzes. When you build finance and lease systems, developers often need to understand journal entries and account titles. Instead of reading a textbook from the beginning, this tool lets you get a feel for what each account title is by answering questions.

Who is it for?

  • Developers who are new to accounting terms
  • People who need to understand journal entries and account titles in a business system
  • Anyone who wants a quick review of account titles

Things to know

  • This is a learning tool and does not replace accounting or tax advice.
  • For actual accounting treatment, follow your company's accounting policies and the judgment of a qualified professional.
