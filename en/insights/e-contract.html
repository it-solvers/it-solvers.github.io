How it works

The sender (the person sending the contract) does four things.

  1. 1

    Upload the contract PDF

    Drag the signature spots directly onto the document. You can move them any time before signing.

  2. 2

    Sign first

    Confirm you have reviewed the contents and sign. Your signature is placed on the chosen spot, and a link for the other party is created.

  3. 3

    Send the link by KakaoTalk

    The link works once and expires after the deadline. The other party confirms their name on their phone, then signs, or declines with a written reason.

  4. 4

    Receive the signed copy and its record

    See the PDF with both signatures, the signing history, and the document fingerprint used to detect tampering, all on one screen.

What makes it easier

It removes the annoyances for people who don't sign many contracts.

The other party only opens a link

No app and no sign-up. They open the link they received on KakaoTalk on their phone and sign right away.

Pay only for what you use

There is no monthly subscription. Buy contracts one at a time or as a bundle.

A record is kept

The signing time, the device used and the document fingerprint are kept as signing history, so you can later check that the document was not altered.

Refusals are recorded too

If the other party declines, their reason is saved and the sender can see it on the contract detail screen.

Who it is for

Built for people who don't want to leave their contracts with someone else's platform.

  • People who want to install it on their own server
  • People tired of paying monthly subscription fees
  • Freelancers and businesses of 1–5 people
