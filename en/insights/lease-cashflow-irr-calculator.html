Lease cash-flow IRR calculator

Estimate a monthly lease payment and annualized IRR from modeled cash flows, including when insurance and vehicle tax are paid. All amounts are shown in U.S. dollars ($).

How it works

  1. The calculator divides the annual rate by 12 and estimates the monthly lease payment using the combined guaranteed and non-guaranteed residual value at the end of the term.
  2. Monthly cash flows include lease payments and the insurance and vehicle-tax amounts collected from the customer.
  3. Insurance is modeled as a full annual payment at delivery and every 12 months afterward. Vehicle tax is prorated for the first year based on the delivery month, then paid annually.
  4. It includes the vehicle-price outflow at delivery and the residual-value inflow at the end of the term. The monthly IRR is found by bisection so the cash flows' net present value equals zero, then annualized as monthly IRR × 12.

The input rate is used to estimate the monthly payment. The cash-flow IRR is calculated separately using the actual modeled timing of insurance and vehicle-tax payments, so it may differ from the input rate.

This is an illustrative cash-flow analysis based on the inputs you provide. It does not represent the accounting treatment or revenue recognition of any particular lease contract.

U.S. guidance: The insurance and vehicle-tax payment timing assumptions come from the Korean version of this calculator. They are modeling assumptions—not U.S. GAAP or U.S. tax rules.

Annualized cash-flow IRR

Total monthly customer bill = base monthly lease payment + monthly insurance collected + monthly vehicle tax collected. The monthly fee is not added to the customer's bill; it is modeled as a monthly cash outflow when calculating IRR.

Annualized IRR is monthly IRR × 12, matching the convention used by the Korean-language calculator. This is a nominal annualization, not an effective annual rate.

Amounts in USD
