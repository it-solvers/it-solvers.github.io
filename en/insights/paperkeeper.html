PaperKeeper (coming soon)

An Android app that scans pages of a paper book, reads the text, and lets you add notes. The Android version is planned for release in November.

It is not available to download yet. We will post details here when it launches.

How it works

  1. 1📷ScanScan one page at a time, or both pages of an open book at once.
  2. 2✂️Clean upRotate, crop, and apply Bright, Grayscale, or Black & white filters.
  3. 3📝Read and annotateRecognize Korean text, search it, and write handwritten notes.
  4. 4📄Save as PDFTurn a whole book into a PDF saved to your Documents folder.

Key features

  • 📚Books, scores, papersGroup pages into one book, whether it is a novel, sheet music, or documents.
  • ✍️Handwritten notesWrite directly on a page with a pen, highlighter, and eraser.
  • 🔍Text searchSearch all recognized text and jump straight to the page.
  • 🛡️Stays on your phoneNo sign-up and no servers. Everything is stored on your phone.
  • Fix recognized textCorrect any misread text yourself and change the text size.
  • Free, no adsFree to use, with no ads.
  • 📖Turn it sideways, like an open bookTurn your phone sideways and it feels like reading a real book or score.
