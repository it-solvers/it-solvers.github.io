PaperKeeper (coming soon)

An Android app that scans pages of a paper book, reads the text, and lets you add notes. The Android version is planned for release in November.

It is not available to download yet. We will post details here when it launches.

How it works

  1. 1ScanScan one page at a time, or both pages of an open book at once.
  2. 2Clean upCurved pages are flattened automatically. Rotate, crop, and apply filters.
  3. 3Read and annotateRecognize Korean text, search it, and write handwritten notes.
  4. 4Save as PDFTurn a whole book into a PDF saved to your Documents folder.

Isn't it just taking photos?

If you only look at the shooting, it's the same as any camera app. The difference is what happens after.

Tips for good scans

The shot matters most. You can find the same tips on the app's home screen.

  • Press the pages flat. Keep your fingers outside the page edge, not on the text or pictures.
  • Fit the whole page in the frame and shoot straight down from above.
  • If the scanner picked the page border wrong, drag the four corners onto the page before tapping Done.
  • Shoot in bright light without shadows so the text stays sharp.
  • Don't use a filter on a blurry page. Retake it from the book viewer instead.

Good to know

  • It is an Android app. It also works on tablets and on foldable phones such as the Galaxy Z Fold.
  • Flattening depends on the photo. Badly distorted photos, or fingers covering the text, have limits, so the app shows scanning tips on the Home screen.
  • The release is planned for November and may change depending on store review.
  • An iPhone (iOS) version is planned for a later release.
