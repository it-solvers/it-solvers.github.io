Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

IT Solvers ("we") respects your privacy and complies with applicable laws, including Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. This policy applies to our website at itsolvers.co.kr.

1. Information we collect and why

We collect information only when you send us an inquiry. There are no user accounts.

  • What we collect: your name (or company or organization name), email address, and the content of your message
  • Why: to read and reply to your inquiry
  • How: through the contact form or an email you send us

You may decline to consent to this collection. If you do, you cannot use the contact form, but you can still email us at itsolvers@naver.com.

2. How long we keep it

We delete your information promptly after we finish replying. If a law requires us to keep it longer, we keep it for that period and then delete it. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

3. Service providers and sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except where the law requires. The contact form passes your message to our inbox through a form-delivery service, Web3Forms. We receive email through a Korean portal mail service (Naver).

4. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This site uses the following services to understand how it is used and to show ads. They may use cookies.

  • Google Analytics: collects anonymous statistics such as visit counts and pages viewed, which we use to improve the site.
  • Google AdSense and other advertising services: third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Display setting: your light or dark mode choice is stored only in your browser and is not sent to us.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as expected.

5. Your rights

You may ask at any time to see, correct, or delete your personal information, to stop its processing, or to withdraw your consent. Email us at the address below and we will act on your request promptly.

6. Security

The contact form sends your message over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only the people who need to read your message can see it, and we delete it once it has served its purpose.

  • Email: itsolvers@naver.com

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the changes and the new effective date on this page.

This policy takes effect on October 21, 2026.
